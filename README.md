# FinSightAI 🌾💰

**AI-Powered Financial Intelligence for Rural Micro-Enterprises**  
*Built for the NABARD Hackathon @ GFF 2026*

---

## 📖 Context
India has millions of rural micro-enterprises, including Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and individual entrepreneurs. These entities often lack formal credit histories, limiting their access to structured financial services. 

However, the rapid adoption of digital payments (such as UPI), along with the availability of market intelligence and other proxy indicators, has created a rich data ecosystem reflecting real-time rural economic activity. **FinSightAI** taps into these diverse datasets to generate predictive insights that enhance financial stability and resilience.

## 🚨 Problem Statement
The current approach to monitoring the financial health of micro-enterprises is largely manual, limiting the ability of institutions and field officers to anticipate risks. 

1. **No Integrated System:** There is no data-driven system that combines financial data with digital transaction signals (e.g., UPI trends), market intelligence, and external factors to predict future cash flows.
2. **Delayed Interventions:** Early warning signs of financial stress are often missed, leading to liquidity issues and increased credit risk.

**The Challenge:** Develop a predictive solution that enables proactive decision-making and timely support for these enterprises.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- **Predict Cash Flows:** Develop an AI/ML-based model to predict cash flows over a 3–6 month horizon.
- **Integrate Multi-Source Data:**
  - *Financial records:* savings, loans, repayments, balances.
  - *Digital proxies:* UPI trends and usage patterns.
  - *Market intelligence:* raw material/commodity prices, demand trends, climate data, and seasonal patterns.
- **Early Warning System:** Identify potential financial stress or risk in micro-enterprises.
- **Actionable Insights:** Provide simple, actionable recommendations for micro-enterprises and field officers to enable corrective actions.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🥇 1. AI Cash Flow Prediction + Explainable AI (Highest Priority)
**What it does:**
- Predicts cash flow for the next 30, 60, and 90 days.
- Shows the probability of a cash shortage.
- **Explainable AI:** Explains exactly *why* the prediction was made to build trust.

> **Example Output:**
> - **Predicted Cash Balance (30 days):** ₹18,500
> - **Risk:** Medium
> - **Reason:** Revenue down 22% | Crop prices fell | Supplier expenses increased

### 🥈 2. Intelligent Risk Flagging & Financial Health Score
**What it does:**
- Generates a **Financial Health Score (0–100)**.
- Detects early warning signs (e.g., expected cash shortage, declining digital payments, delayed supplier payments, high expense-to-income ratio).
- Prioritizes businesses by risk level (Low / Medium / High).

> **Example Output:**
> - **Financial Health Score:** 74/100
> - **Risk:** High
> - **Alert:** "Cash deficit likely within 15 days."

### 🥉 3. AI Recommendation & Intervention Engine
**What it does:** 
Instead of only identifying problems, it recommends proactive solutions.

> **Example Recommendations:**
> - *"Delay non-essential inventory purchases."*
> - *"Apply for a ₹50,000 working capital loan."*
> - *"Collect pending customer payments before the next procurement cycle."*
> - *"Enroll in a government subsidy scheme."*

## 💻 Deliverables
A working prototype (web interface) designed for two primary user groups:

**For Micro-Enterprises:**
- Simple data entry (savings/loans, income, expenses)
- Real-time risk alerts
- Actionable suggestions

**For Field Officers / Lenders:**
- List of micro-enterprises and their current risk status
- Detailed enterprise profiles with cash flow forecast views
- Comprehensive Risk Panel

**Additional Integrations:**
- **Sector-specific risk** (Dairy, Poultry, Food Processing, Handicrafts, Rural Retail).
- **Climate & Market risk** (Weather shocks, commodity price fluctuations).
- **Offline / Low-Network capability.**
- **Multilingual Support.**

---

## 🌟 Value Creation for NABARD

1. **Enhanced Credit Flow:** By generating reliable cash-flow forecasts and risk profiles, the platform strengthens banks' credit appraisal capabilities, leading to improved access to formal credit for underserved rural enterprises.
2. **Credit-led Rural Development:** Acts as a link between grant-based interventions and institutional finance, helping enterprises demonstrate repayment capacity to avail formal credit for business expansion rather than relying solely on grants.
3. **Digital Public Good:** Serves as a common digital infrastructure for enterprise profiling, cash-flow assessment, and risk monitoring.
4. **Better Outcomes for Beneficiaries:** NABARD-supported beneficiaries receive actionable insights into business performance and market risks for informed decision-making.
